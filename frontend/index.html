Why RecruitAI?

Everything a recruiter needs to screen hundreds of resumes in minutes.

📄

Smart Resume Parsing

Automatically extracts name, email, phone, skills, education, and experience from PDF and Word resumes.

🧠

AI Matching Engine

Uses TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity to measure how well each resume matches a job description.

🏆

Automatic Ranking

Candidates are ranked 1 to N by match score. Top scorers are auto-shortlisted for recruiter review.

☁️

Cloud-Based Storage

All resumes and results stored securely in the cloud. Access from anywhere, anytime.

📊

Recruiter Dashboard

Visual dashboard showing match scores, skills breakdown, shortlist management, and analytics.

⚡

Bulk Upload

Upload and screen multiple resumes at once. Process 100 resumes in seconds, not hours.
